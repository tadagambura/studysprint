# StudySprint

**Exam countdowns and a revision timetable that plans itself**

> #13 of 15 · Wave 2: Recurring keepers · Used: Daily in exam season · Easy · Revenue potential: Medium · Habits, Health & Learning

## What it is

Enter your exams, subjects and topics, mark how confident you feel, and StudySprint builds a realistic revision timetable that gives more time to weak topics and closer exams. Includes a focus timer and countdowns.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Students search for revision timetables every year and share study content heavily. Retention is strong for months during revision season, and premium plans suit both students and parents.

## Who it's for

GCSE, A-level, college and university students; adults taking professional exams.

**The problem:** Students know when their exams are, but not how to split their time across subjects and topics.

## Version one

- Add exams, subjects and topics with confidence levels
- Auto-generated timetable, weighted by how close and how weak
- Focus timer (sprint sessions)
- Tick off sessions; progress per subject
- Exam countdowns

**Example:** A student enters five exams, marks two weak topics, and gets a daily plan from January to June.

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Sprint complete: the stickman bursts through a paper wall
- Break time: he flops onto a beanbag
- Weak topic improved: the brain lifts a dumbbell
- Exam day: he suits up like a superhero
- Skipped session: the timetable gives a gentle side-eye, then quietly reschedules itself

**Line we don't cross:** Completely calm during a focus session.
